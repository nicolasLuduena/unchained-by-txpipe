# Handoff design system

Scope: the design system only, in `design/`: tokens, components, logos, brand book and one catalog page. No app or wallet flow is built. Mode: Operate, because the system serves working wallet and console screens.

Audience and constraints live in PRODUCT.md. Screens are dark, with no white or near-white grounds.

Chosen direction: Cupón in the Sarga scheme, approved sketch `.impeccable/mocks/decision/n-sarga.html`.

Open: how far a host wallet may restyle the embedded screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every order is a coupon that validates, tears or voids, and nothing disappears. It refuses the fintech default of soft rounded cards with coloured pills.

OWN-WORLD: Old navy serge ground, a lighter coupon stock, buff text, honey amber for figures and the one action. Square corners, hairline ruled fields, condensed caps on coupon heads, a dashed perforation, stamped states with a word in a ruled box.

STORY: A reader of the catalog sees one coherent object language and can assemble any wallet or console screen from it, knowing what each state looks like.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Catalog opens on the Handoff lockup and a full Coupon at real size beside the palette, so the signature object leads. Navigation to tokens and components sits at the top.

FORM: Jet-age ticket wallet, chosen by the user over the rolled direction, position 1 of the shown hand. seed:69691f3b880d4188. Signature move: the perforation and stamp, where a coupon tears once on fill and states stamp on rather than disappear.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
