**Three sentences to paste**

1. "This is a market that grows once it is regulated: Brazil's crowdfunding channel, once the CVM opened it to token offers, went from under R$200 million in 2023 to R$3.9 billion in 2025, and Japan's public security tokens doubled to ¥333 billion in a single year."
2. "Argentina just did what Japan did in 2020 and Dubai did in 2025: it let retail buy tokenized property first, and resale came later, more than three and a half years later in Japan and about nine months later in Dubai."
3. "The piece nobody built is the shared resale layer: of 82 public security tokens issued in Japan, 8 trade on START, Switzerland's SDX gave back its exchange licence after banks did not adapt their systems to use it, and Dubai's resale lives in one app; Handoff puts one resale layer inside the wallets investors already use."

**Do not say**

- "US$16 trillion" as if measured. It is a 2022 BCG and ADDX projection; the measured figure is US$39.36B.
- "The EU DLT Pilot was a success", or "it died". ESMA found limited uptake, and the cap is now being raised, not abolished.
- "SDX shows what a licensed secondary market looks like." SDX Trading's last day was 30 May 2025 and the licence was returned.
- "BEE4 is a sandbox venue." It left the sandbox with definitive licences in 2025.
- "Drex is the rail." The platform was reported shut down on 10 Nov 2025.
- "Regulation caused the Treasury boom." It began in 2023 on rising rates, before the 2025 and 2026 SEC steps.
- "R$3.9 billion of tokens." That is all crowdfunding; the token share was not found.
- "Dubai sold out in under two minutes" as a fact. The first sale took "within just one day"; the faster claim could not be opened at source.
- "Compliant." Per #1, say "built to the structure of Título XXII and operated by a PSAV".
- "Nobody built resale." Per #1, Volsmart has. The claim is no shared, cross-PSAV layer.

## Part 3. Jurisdiction cards

Four ticket premises turned out stale and are corrected below: BEE4 is out of the sandbox, Drex was switched off, SDX Trading closed, and the UK sandbox is wholesale infrastructure rather than a retail venue.

### Brazil

- **Rule and date:** CVM Resolution 88 of 2022 covers crowdfunding; [Conjur, 2026-02-04](https://www.conjur.com.br/2026-fev-04/crowdfunding-no-brasil-proximos-passos//?print=1) calls CVM's authorization of token offers through platforms the turning point (date of that step not found). Sandbox: CVM Resolution 29 of 11 May 2021 and Deliberation 874 of 30 Sep 2021 ([BEE4](https://bee4.com.br/en/who-we-are/)). Drex: pilot only; the central bank told participants on 4 Nov 2025 it would switch the platform off on 10 Nov, reported by [Finsiders citing Blocknews](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/pagamentos/drex/bc-desliga-plataforma-drex-e-diz-que-criara-nova-infraestr), not confirmed by the bank in that article.
- **Who may issue:** Crowdfunding: issuers with revenue up to R$40M, offers up to R$15M (Conjur). BEE4 sandbox: revenue up to R$300M, issues up to R$100M, up to 10 issuers; FÁCIL target R$500M and R$300M ([Capital Aberto, 2025-03-26](https://www.capitalaberto.com.br/alem-do-capital/2025/03/patricia-stille-cofundadora-da-bee4-fala-no-alem-do-capital)).
- **Who may run secondary:** A CVM-licensed organized OTC market. BEE4 got a definitive licence in April 2025 after a depositary licence in January 2025, "a primeira a sair do Sandbox" ([Capital Aberto, 2025-04-11](https://capitalaberto.com.br/regulacao/2025/04/bee4-recebe-licenca-definitiva-para-balcao-organizado)).
- **Transfer restrictions:** Not found (CVM text returned 404).
- **Investor caps:** R$20,000 a year across platforms, reform pending ([Uai, 2026-08-24](https://www.uai.com.br/?p=169476)); exceptions for qualified investors not read.
- **Sandbox end date:** BEE4's notice gives 7 Jun 2022 to 6 Jun 2026 ([BEE4](https://bee4.com.br/en/who-we-are/)); that page conflicts with its [about page](https://bee4.com.br/en/about-us/), which reports definitive licences in 2025. The licence date governs.
- **Flagship issues:** Engravida (first listing, 2022); Plamev Pet raised R$5.42M against a R$6.4M target ([InfoMoney, 2023-10-17](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=2266224)); Mais Mu and Eletron also listed.
- **Before and after:** BEE4 first session 18,475 tokens, R$331,775 ([InfoMoney, 2022-09-29](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=1945270)); near R$2M over 50-plus sessions by Oct 2023 ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=2266224)). Current BEE4 volume not found. Crowdfunding in comparison 1 below.
- **Venues:** BEE4, with Itaú and Genial integrated in 2024 ([BEE4](https://bee4.com.br/en/about-us/)); four listed companies in March 2025.
- **Hours:** Weekly session, Wednesdays 12:00 to 20:00 at launch ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=1945270)); still one weekly session in March 2025 ([Capital Aberto](https://www.capitalaberto.com.br/alem-do-capital/2025/03/patricia-stille-cofundadora-da-bee4-fala-no-alem-do-capital)). Current hours not found.

### Germany

- **Rule and date:** eWpG, dated 3 Jun 2021, in force 10 Jun 2021, last amended 4 Feb 2026 ([gesetze-im-internet](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ewpg/BJNR142310021.html)).
- **Who may issue:** Issuers of bearer bonds, and of shares within the limits in § 1; the issuer names the registrar under § 16(2), otherwise the issuer itself counts as registrar.
- **Who may run secondary:** The eWpG sets up registers, not venues. Trading runs on DLT Pilot venues such as 360X and 21X (EU card). Registrars: 8 firms in BaFin's category on 29 Sep 2026 ([Bitbond](https://www.bitbond.com/resources/tokenization-companies-germany)).
- **Transfer restrictions:** § 25: transfer needs re-registration on the holder's instruction plus agreement between the parties.
- **Investor caps:** None found in the eWpG sections read.
- **Sandbox end date:** None. Permanent statute.
- **Flagship issues:** Siemens €60M, one year, public blockchain, sold direct to DekaBank, DZ Bank and Union Investment ([Siemens, 2023-02-14](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-issues-first-digital-bond-blockchain)). Siemens €300M, one year, under eWpG ([Siemens, 2024-09-04](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:f54c58b6-988c-45d7-94f4-eb6de14ccd2a/HQCOPR202409026988EN.pdf)).
- **Before and after:** 17 crypto securities at 15 Dec 2022 ([Bundestag, 2022-12-20](https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-927634)). Current count not found.
- **Venues:** 360X reported the first secondary trade in the €300M bond, between Dekabank and Union Investment ([Ledger Insights, 2025-08-11](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/siemens-digital-bond-traded-on-regulated-trading-venue-360x/)).
- **Hours:** Not found.
- **Lesson:** A statute gets blue-chip primary issuance. The first regulated secondary trade in Siemens' one-year bond was reported about 11 months after issue.

### Switzerland

- **Rule and date:** DLT Act: civil-law parts 1 Feb 2021, remainder 1 Aug 2021 ([PwC (excerpt)](https://www.pwc.ch/en/insights/regulation/partial-entry-swiss-dlt-act.html)).
- **Who may issue:** Not found as an eligibility rule. Observed issuers: SIX, UBS, World Bank, cantons and cities ([SIX, 2024-05-21](https://www.six-group.com/en/newsroom/media-releases/2024/20240521-six-sdx-digital-bonds-milestone.html)).
- **Who may run secondary:** FINMA-licensed DLT trading facility. BX Digital was the first, and is a small facility subject to thresholds, with values not given ([FINMA, 2025-03-18](https://www.finma.ch/en/news/2025/03/20250318-mm-dlt-handelssystem/)).
- **Transfer restrictions:** Ledger-based securities are asserted and transferred only through the ledger ([PwC (excerpt)](https://www.pwc.ch/en/insights/regulation/partial-entry-swiss-dlt-act.html)).
- **Investor caps:** None found. The licence can admit retail participants directly ([Goldblum, 2026-06-10](https://goldblum.ch/crypto/dlt-trading-facility/)); BX Digital's downstream settlement is aimed at supervised participants, usually banks (FINMA).
- **Sandbox end date:** None. Permanent.
- **Flagship issues:** SIX CHF 150M, CHF 100M of it digital ([SIX, 2021-11-18](https://www.six-group.com/en/newsroom/media-releases/2021/20211118-six-sdx-digital-bond.html)). UBS CHF 375M, three years, 2.33% ([UBS, 2022-11-03](https://www.ubs.com/global/en/media/display-page-ndp/en-20221103-digital-bond.html)). World Bank CHF 200M (SIX, 2024).
- **Before and after:** CHF 100M digital in Nov 2021 to above CHF 1B by May 2024. Trading volumes not found.
- **Venues:** SDX Trading: last trading day 30 May 2025, licence returned voluntarily; SDX continues as a depository ([cash.ch, 2025-11-27](https://www.cash.ch/news/top-news/six-hat-borsenlizenz-fur-digitalborse-sdx-zuruckgegeben-885862)). SIX Swiss Exchange trades listed digital bonds; BX Digital.
- **Hours:** Not found.
- **Lesson:** A licence does not create a market. SIX's CEO said customers faced "einen sehr grossen Aufwand" to adapt, and banks did not use the venue. Liquidity goes where participants already are.

### EU

- **Rule and date:** Regulation 2022/858, applying from 23 Mar 2023 ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R0858); [ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-06/ESMA75-117376770-460_Report_on_the_functioning_and_review_of_the_DLTR_-_Art.14.pdf)).
- **Who may issue:** Shares of issuers under €500M market cap; bonds under €1B issue size; UCITS-type funds under €500M (Art. 3).
- **Who may run secondary:** Holders of a specific permission for a DLT MTF or DLT TSS.
- **Transfer restrictions:** Not found.
- **Investor caps:** None per investor found. Venue cap: €6B aggregate, transition at €9B (Art. 3). Direct retail access possible under Art. 4(2) conditions.
- **Sandbox end date:** Permission lasts up to six years (Art. 8(11)). The Commission's 4 Dec 2025 package would raise the cap to €100B and end time limits ([William Fry, 2026-02-04](https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/european-market-integration-dlt-in-the-eu/)). The Council wants €300B; Lagarde objected ([Ledger Insights, 2026-10-12](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/eu-finance-ministers-wants-dlt-pilot-regime-cap-raised-to-e300bn-lagarde-objects/)). Not adopted in sources read.
- **Flagship issues:** Not found with sizes.
- **Before and after:** Zero authorised and four applications in April 2024 ([DLA Piper, 2025-07-10](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/2025/07/esma-suggests-amendments-to-the-dlt-pilot-regime-to-make-it-permanent-and-attractive)); 3 at 31 May 2025; 8 on the list now.
- **Venues:** CSD Prague (11 Oct 2024), 21X (3 Dec 2024), 360X (29 Apr 2025), Axiology (9 Jul 2025), LISE (13 Oct 2025), Securitize Europe (26 Nov 2025), Seturion (16 Oct 2026), Bit2Me STX (27 Oct 2026) ([ESMA list](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-01/Authorised_DLT_Market_Infrastructures.pdf)).
- **Hours:** Not found.
- **Lesson:** Caps plus frictions held it back. ESMA names thresholds, interoperability and central bank money access, with "minimal live trading". Authorisations are not liquidity; no volume figure was found. Argentina's five-venue limit is a smaller version of the same design question.

### United Kingdom

- **Rule and date:** Financial Services and Markets Act 2023 (Digital Securities Sandbox) Regulations 2023, in force 8 Jan 2024 ([legislation.gov.uk (excerpt)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2023/1398/made)). Open to applications from 30 Sep 2024 ([Willkie, 2024-10-09](https://willkie.com/publications/2024/10/bank-of-england-and-uk-financial-conduct-authority-open-digital-securities-sandbox-to-applicants)).
- **Who may issue:** Scope covers equities, corporate and government bonds, money market instruments, fund units and emissions allowances; derivatives and unbacked crypto are out ([Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability/digital-securities-sandbox)).
- **Who may run secondary:** A sandbox entrant at Gate 2, as a depository, alone or with a trading venue.
- **Transfer restrictions:** Not found.
- **Investor caps:** Per-investor not found. Firm limits at go-live: gilts £600M (up to £1.25B on request), corporate bonds £900M (up to £1.5B), ABS £600M, money market £300M, equity per stock (Willkie). Final policy moved to limit ranges ([FCA PS24/12](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps24-12-digital-securities-sandbox-joint-policy-statement-final-guidance)).
- **Sandbox end date:** 8 Jan 2029, extendable by HM Treasury (Bank of England).
- **Flagship issues:** Not found with sizes.
- **Before and after:** No entrants before Dec 2024; 17 past Gate 1, 3 past Gate 2 by 2 Nov 2026 (HSBC 13 Jul, ClearToken 18 Sep, LSEG B3 23 Oct) ([dashboard](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability/digital-securities-sandbox/digital-securities-sandbox-dashboard)). Sixteen firms in [May 2026](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/news/2026/may/fca-and-boe-set-out-shared-vision-for-tokenisation-in-uk-wholesale-markets).
- **Venues:** None confirmed trading; volume not found.

- **Lesson:** Same time-boxed shape as Argentina, but aimed at wholesale market infrastructure. First Gate 2 came about 19 months after the first Gate 1.

### United States

- **Rule and date:** No token statute. SEC staff statement, 28 Jan 2026: format "does not affect application of the federal securities laws" ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826)). Nasdaq tokenized trading approved, March 2026 ([Dechert, 2026-03-25](https://www.dechert.com/knowledge/onpoint/2026/3/sec-issues-landmark-interpretation-on-the-application-of-federal.html)). Innovation Exemption, 17 Sep 2026 ([SEC 2026-90](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment)).
- **Who may issue:** Registered or exempt issuers. Rule 506(b) ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/private-placements-rule-506b)); Regulation A up to US$20M or US$75M in 12 months ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/regulation)).
- **Who may run secondary:** ATSs, exchanges, and now Tokenized Securities Venues: a US person, permissioned and centrally managed, so immutable DEXs do not qualify ([Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/sec-s-innovation-exemption-opens-path-tokenized-stock-trading)).
- **Transfer restrictions:** 506(b) buyers hold restricted securities. Third-party tokens need 30 days' notice to the issuer, who may object (Davis Polk).
- **Investor caps:** 506(b): at most 35 non-accredited per 90 days. Regulation A Tier 2: limits on non-accredited investors ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/regulation)). TSV stock caps: Tier 1, 75 symbols and 0.25% of average daily volume; Tier 2, 250 symbols and 2.5% (Davis Polk).
- **Sandbox end date:** Exemption runs through 17 Sep 2031. DTC relief came by December 2025 no-action letter.
- **Flagship issues:** BUIDL, US$5M minimum under Rule 506(c) ([Business Wire, 2024-03-20 (excerpt)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240320771318/en/BlackRock-Launches-Its-First-Tokenized-Fund-BUIDL-on-the-Ethereum-Network)); BENJI; USDY. Sizes in Part 2.
- **Before and after:** Comparison 3 below. DTCC service live 28 Oct 2026 with 20 participants signed ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/press-releases/2026/dtcc-tokenization-service-goes-live)); production trades with 30-plus firms on 15 Jul 2026 ([DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/news/2026/july/15/dtcc-turns-tokenization-into-reality)).
- **Venues:** tZERO; INX, acquired by Republic ([Republic (excerpt)](https://republic.com/note/updates/inx-acquisition-closes)); Securitize Markets; Nasdaq. Current ATS volumes not found: the figures seen came from unverifiable sites and were dropped.
- **Hours:** Not found for ATSs. A TSV must halt when the primary exchange halts the stock (SEC).
- **Lesson:** Biggest numbers, led by Treasury funds such as BUIDL with its US$5M minimum, not retail securities. tZERO's thin-liquidity record: three weeks after its January 2019 launch, 9,000 to 35,000 TZROP a day by company count out of 26M issued, price from US$8 to as low as US$3 ([CoinDesk, 2019-02-15](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2019/02/15/tzero-is-live-but-volume-is-light-and-its-tokens-price-is-down-sharply)). That is 2019 data; say so.

### Hong Kong

- **Sandbox end date:** Secondary framework is a pilot; end date not found.

About HK$10B, Nov 2025, with tokenised central bank money ([Linklaters](https://www.linklaters.com/en/about-us/news-and-deals/deals/2025/november/linklaters-advises-on-the-hong-kong-sar-governments-largest-ever-digital-green-bonds-offering)).

### Singapore

**Best "Argentina just did what X did" analogues**

1. **Japan, 2020:** retail real-estate tokens first, secondary later, and thin liquidity once it arrived.
2. **Dubai, 2025:** same asset class, retail tickets and a pilot, with resale added as a second phase.
3. **UK, 2024:** the same time-boxed shape with trading inside the box, though wholesale rather than retail.

Brazil stays the venue comparable rather than an analogue: closest neighbour, and proof a sandbox venue can graduate.
